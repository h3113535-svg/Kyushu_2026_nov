# Kyushu_2026_nov v1.11.15 Changes

## Scope
- Daily itinerary title wrapping only.
- No itinerary content changes.
- No Hero changes.
- No Firebase content update.

## What changed
1. Daily titles now wrap by semantic itinerary phrases instead of arbitrary character positions.
2. `→`, `＋`, `・`, and `/` stay attached to the preceding phrase, preventing separators from appearing alone at the beginning of a line.
3. Titles using `｜` are rendered as intentional two-tier editorial headings, giving D5/D6/D8 cleaner travel-book style line breaks.
4. English/mixed terms such as `Canal City` and `Villa＋自炊` are kept together where possible.
5. Version/cache bumped from `1.11.14` / `11114` to `1.11.15` / `11115`.
